I'm a Computer Engineer who loves building where intelligent systems meet security. Most recently at Cognizant, I architected "Aerotrace," a production Pydantic AI multi-agent system for a 200+ aircraft cargo airline that cut compliance reporting from 3 days to under 30 seconds and slashed cost per report from $800+ to under $1.

My path spans industry (Agentic AI & data engineering, ML for cybersecurity, zero-trust identity, enterprise full-stack) and research, fine-tuning vision-language models for accessible navigation at the H2X Lab, and studying RLHF failure modes and mechanistic interpretability as an AI Safety & Alignment Technical Fellow.

I also lead as President of the BU Cybersecurity Association, running hands-on workshops in penetration testing and threat analysis. Always chasing the next hard problem at the intersection of AI and security.
